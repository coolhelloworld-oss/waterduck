# 🦆 Waterduck — The future of the Browser

**Waterduck** is a radical, community-driven browser project. While others are closing their gates and rejecting public contributions, we are opening our doors wide to the entire open-source world. We believe the future of the web should be shaped by everyone — not just a select group of maintainers.

---

## 🚀 Our Philosophy: Truly Open & Polyglot

Waterduck breaks the rule that a browser must be limited to just one or two languages. In our ecosystem, every language is first-class and can be used across the entire project — from the core engine to the frontend UI.

## 🗺️ Roadmap & The Fork Strategy

To ensure maximum stability while maintaining absolute community freedom, Waterduck follows a highly strategic two-phase launch plan:

1. **🔄 The Sync Phase (Right Now):** We are currently tracking and synchronizing our codebase with upstream Ladybird development. This allows us to inherit their engine updates leading up to the official Alpha release.
2. **💥 The Hard Fork (Summer 2026):** Once the Alpha milestone is reached this summer, Waterduck will officially execute a hard fork. From this exact moment onward, our codebases will split completely.
3. **🔓 The Open Gate:** Post-fork, we will immediately unlock public Pull Requests for the global community and begin integrating our multi-language infrastructure.

---

## ⚡ Why Waterduck?

* **True Open-Source PRs:** We will never close public pull requests. Every single contribution from the community is welcome, reviewed, and valued.
* **Write in Any Language:** No restrictions. Contribute to any part of the browser using the language you know best.
* **Full Cross-Platform:** Built from day one to run natively on Linux, macOS, and Windows.
* **Independent Engine:** No Chromium, no WebKit. We are building an independent engine from scratch, powered by the global community.
